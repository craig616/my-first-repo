UX

Fridge: who's eating and search live in a tray at the bottom, under the thumb; "have 5 of 7" sits on every card, so a glance answers "do we have it?".

Sofa: a fan of cards to flick through, with the rest of the drawer filed below and divider tabs for the main ingredient.

Propped up: one card, 30 px step text, timers hung on a rail above it and Back/Next as two 92 px blocks.

UI

The walnut is the drawer and the worktop; the cards are warm cream paper with a faint grain, index-card rules and a red margin line. Photos are taped on, slightly crooked, as if cut from a magazine.

Jost does all the work. Caveat appears only for notes: Craig in graphite, Mary in blue-black ink. Stamps are oxblood ink, and yolk is kept for the one thing to press.

Content

Quiet and plain: "Something for tonight", "Start cooking", "Next · grill". Amounts in grams where useful, oven in °C fan only.

"Needs checking" is a dashed pencil circle in Jost, never a warning: the cod amount stays missing, with what the reel shows beside it. Missing items say "need" in stamp red; optional ones say so.

Risks

Paper and tape can tip into scrapbook kitsch if the stamps and notes multiply; they need a strict budget of one stamp and one or two notes a card.

Handwritten notes may be read as recipe facts, and invented-looking notes need real authorship. Cream cards on walnut are bright at night in a dark kitchen.
