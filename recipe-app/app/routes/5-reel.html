UI

The 9:16 video runs edge to edge, as in the Creme app. Text sits only where a scrim makes it safe: a dark gradient at the top for the title, a near-solid walnut fade at the foot for the card text, and smoked-glass pills (74% walnut and blur) for every control on the picture.

The sheet is the one solid surface, in walnut card brown. Yolk is kept for the two things that matter in the moment: Start cooking and the running bake timer. Step progress borrows the story bars at the top. Jost only.

Content

Short and plain, in the creator's own frame: "Lisa · @thelisafeed" is the first thing under the title, and "Watch original" credits the source. The ▶ jumps are chapters named for the job ("Marinate", "Grill"), with the timer beside the timestamp.

The cod is marked with a dashed circle and a quiet brass "Needs checking": "Amount not stated. Two pieces in the video." Missing servings read "Servings not stated" rather than a guess. Oven times are in °C fan.

Risks

Burned-in captions sit around 70% of the way down most Reels. Every layout keeps them under a solid sheet or a heavy scrim, but a creator who captions higher up will clash with the title.

Video-first is only as good as the footage: the minestrone is a photo carousel with no video, and some step moments are never filmed. A full-screen feed shows one recipe at a time, which is slower for comparing than a grid.
