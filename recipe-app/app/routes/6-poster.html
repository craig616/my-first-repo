Route 6 Poster

The idea: the kitchen as a market sign. Big blocks of yolk, plaster, brass and walnut; cooking times printed huge; one thing to read from across the room, and very few words.

Video: the card in focus plays a silent, looping preview, marked “Silent preview”. On the recipe the photo block is the video, still until tapped, with sound on tap; ▶ times in the method jump straight into it.

Yolk · tonight, now Plaster · the method Brass · have it Steel · other timers Walnut · ground Dashed · needs checking

UX

Fridge: search sits at the bottom as a solid plaster block, so a thumb reaches it with the door held open, and every recipe row says “have 11 of 13” in plain words.

Propped up: cook mode is three stacked signs: the clock, the step, the way on. Back and Next are 118 px tall and the full width, made to be hit with a knuckle.

Sofa: one Tonight block, then a board of times, so choosing starts with how long you have.

UI

Numbers are set in Big Shoulders Display at 54 to 118 px; words are Jost at 500 to 800. Colour carries meaning: yolk is tonight and now, plaster is the method and the way forward, brass is what you have, steel is the other timer.

Photos are cropped hard into blocks with no gradients or gloss. The phone stays dark walnut, so the yolk block is the brightest thing in a dim kitchen.

Content

Loud shapes, quiet words: “Something for tonight”, “Start cooking”, “Next”. Times come first because they decide supper. Marinating is shown as “+ 1 hr”, so the 22 never misleads.

Anything unconfirmed has a dashed outline: the hollow “2?” for servings, the cod amount, and a “?” where a time isn’t given. Steps are trimmed to one idea each, in grams, fan °C and hob numbers.

Risks

Blocks this big leave little room. The board shows eight recipes, not fifty, and long titles run to three lines.

A colour code has to be learned. If yolk turns up too often it stops meaning “now”.

The hollow numeral is subtle, so it needs testing to show it reads as “not sure” and not as decoration.
