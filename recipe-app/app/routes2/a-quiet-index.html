What this option is good at

  • Calm to scan. Everything sits on one walnut ground, so the eye moves by size and spacing alone, and long recipe names simply wrap.
  • Fast in the kitchen. Cook mode is plain text at a large size, with the running timer and its finish time always in view.
  • Easy to build and keep consistent. Two weights, one font and a short list of spacings mean new screens look right first time.

Watch out for

  • Appetite. With photos kept small, the food can feel secondary; the lead photo and recipe plate must stay generous.
  • Rhythm drifting into sameness. Without lines or fields, spacing has to be disciplined, or sections run together.
  • Too quiet for some. Light weights on dark need enough size and contrast; nothing should drop below 13px.
