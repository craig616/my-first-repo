What this option is good at

  • Appetite first. You choose with your eyes; the Best match photo and the Reel do the persuading before a word is read.
  • One surface for the words. The sheet is the only container, so text never sits in boxes inside boxes.
  • Cook mode feels like the dish. The blurred photo keeps you in the recipe while the step and the timer stay sharp and calm.

Watch out for

  • Reel captions and pale plates. Every image needs a scrim and a crop chosen per recipe; burned-in text has to be cropped or covered by the sheet.
  • Weak or missing photos. A dull frame grab fills the screen too. We need a fallback that is a calm walnut field, not a placeholder.
  • Less on the first screen. The big image pushes ingredients and method below the fold; the sheet must be quick to pull up.
