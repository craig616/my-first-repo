Round 2 · Option C

Kitchen Journal

Warm and editorial, like a well-kept cookbook: light serif titles, photos set like plates, and soft colour fields in place of lines.

Images
Larger thumbnails throughout; the recipe photo set with a margin, like a plate in a book.
Type
Newsreader Light for titles, the cook-mode step and the story. Jost for everything you act on.
Separation
Soft muted fields (sage, clay, plaster) mark sections. Full-width bands, never cards.

What this option is good at

  • It feels like ours: the serif titles and the story read like a kitchen notebook, which suits a quiet, Nigel Slater-like voice.
  • Colour fields give each part of the landing a clear place without a single line, and they double as the timer row and the word sheet.
  • The two-column recipe puts ingredients beside the method, so both are visible before you scroll, as in a good cookbook.

Watch out for

  • Three fields plus walnut is the limit; any more and the bands start to read as cards, so they stay full-width and few.
  • Serif steps are lovely but larger; very long steps in cook mode will need the text to scroll above the fixed Back and Next.
  • The narrow ingredients column wraps long names; on small phones it may need to stack above the method.
