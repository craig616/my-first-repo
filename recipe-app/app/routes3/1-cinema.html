Images
Edge to edge with square corners. The Reel plays behind the landing and the recipe, then blurs into the dark in cook mode.

Good at

  • The food sells itself. You choose from moving pictures, which is how these recipes were found in the first place.
  • Each screen has one job and one yolk moment, so the eye never has to hunt.
  • The change of room is total: from a bright, moving film to a dark screen with one sentence and a clock you can read from the hob.

Watch out for

  • Video everywhere is heavy on battery and data; the landing should fall back to the still when the phone is on low power.
  • Text over moving images needs strong scrims, and some Reels are bright and busy. Every hero will need a check.
  • A pager shows one dish at a time, which is slower for comparing; the filmstrip and discovery shelves have to carry the browsing.
