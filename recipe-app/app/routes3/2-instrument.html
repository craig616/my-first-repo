Built from: round 1 Index (its typography, the search bar at the bottom, the step by step), the change of room into cook mode, long names designed for, and filters kept behind the search.

Good at

  1. Answers “when will we eat?” before anything else; the ready-by column makes the choice for a weeknight.
  2. Reads at arm’s length. One margin, one number style, nothing decorative; it is easy to scan at the fridge.
  3. A real change of room. The cook screen loses the images and the colour, and only the countdown is lit.

Watch out for

  1. Can feel cool on the sofa; the food is small here, so the photos must be well cropped to do their work.
  2. Times must be honest. Ready-by is only useful if prep and marinating times are right for every recipe.
  3. The mono could turn technical; keep it to numbers and never use it for names.
