Editorial, this week’s issue

A dark, matte magazine that changes with the week: the box arrives on Tuesday, so fresh things lead, and cupboard cooking takes over by Friday. Choosing reads like opening the new issue; cooking turns the page quiet.

Built from: the fine type of Hers and the focused steps of Apple News; the highlighted meta line and meals by time of day (2 · Cookbook); the shortlist we build together (4 · Counter); full-bleed key images (5 · Reel); the bottom search and the atmosphere-to-focus transition (1 · Index); the sentence builder.

Colour
Ink-blue black and paper-dark, warm off-white type, clay for the meta line. Yolk only for the one key action on each screen.
Type
Fraunces 200/300 at display optical size for headlines and standfirsts, Jost 300/400 for the working parts. Italic does the emphasis that bold would.
Images
Square-cut and full-bleed, fading into the page under the headline. Small portrait thumbnails in the lists, like a contents page.
Structure
An issue: masthead, a sentence that sets tonight’s brief, one lead spread, then sections by time of day and type of meal. The recipe is an article; cooking is one step at a time.

Good at

  • The lead spread makes one clear suggestion with a reason in a human voice, so choosing feels like being told, not filtering.
  • The sentence builder holds mood and ingredients in a single line you can read aloud, with the filters out of sight.
  • The biggest change of room of the four routes: a rich, image-led issue becomes a plain page with one step on it.

Watch out for

  • Light serifs at weight 200 need size and contrast; below 16px the Fraunces must step up to 300.
  • The lead spread uses a lot of the first screen, so only three more picks show before scrolling.
  • The weekly issue needs real editorial copy (standfirsts, tips) for each recipe, or it reads as a template.
