2 · Data

What the app knows

The recipe is the centre. Your household settings, the pantry and the season shape what's shown. Your cooking writes back as logs and versions.

Recipe

  • id · title · sources (Instagram, website)
  • creator · media (video, hero, transcript)
  • servings · prep / cook / total time
  • course main · lunch · side · baking…
  • mood comforting · light · spicy…
  • larder fresh · cupboard · mixed · perishables
  • seasons · served · heft · method
  • cuisine · diet · contains meat · allergens · equipment
  • status ok · needs checking · current version
  • ingredient lines name · grams/ml · prep · group
  • steps text · ⏱ timer · ▶ video moment · uses
→ has many versions, cook logs, list entries

Household

  • Craig · Mary
  • diet pescatarian together · Craig meat alone
  • kitchen fan oven · 1–9 induction
  • rhythm fresh box on Tuesday
  • staples salt, pepper, oils, water

Our list

  • recipe · added by · when
  • reactions Craig ♥ · Mary ♥
  • agreed items rise first

Cook log

  • date · who cooked
  • it was · but it · next time · we'll
→ can create a Version · feeds taste learning

Version

  • v1 = as imported
  • changes · created from which log
  • the original always one tap away

Pantry

  • ingredient · fridge / cupboard / freezer
  • source box · receipt · photo · manual
  • use-by estimate

Reference

  • ingredient vocabulary (167 names)
  • UK seasonality calendar
  • favourites per person

4 · Getting recipes in

From a saved Reel to a recipe you can cook

Scripts do the heavy lifting for free. Sonnet does the writing. Every gap is marked honestly.

Source

Instagram "food" collection

533 saved posts listed once, captions saved. Sorted into full, partial, spoken, not a recipe, carousel.

state.csv · URL list in Obsidian
↓
Script · no AI

collect.py

Video at 720p (one request per post, at most 50 a run and 2 runs a day), audio extracted on the Mac, cover image, frame sheets, hero candidates.

On the Mac · no AI cost
