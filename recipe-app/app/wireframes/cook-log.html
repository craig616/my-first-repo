Cook log · write it as a story

When you finish cook mode, the log appears as a short story with blanks. Context fills itself in (the dish, the day, who's eating); you tap the rest from a few suggested words, or type your own. The finished story becomes the cook log entry, and "Next time…" can become the next version of the recipe. No stars or scores: the words carry the rating.

"Next time" changes are real edits. When they map to an amount, time or step ("halve the sugar"), the app offers to apply them as v2, already ticked. Untick to keep them as a note only.

"We'll make it again soon" puts it back on our list for later. "Not for us" quietly lowers it in suggestions.

Across the app, these stories feed a quiet "Cooked lately" and help the suggestions learn what you love, without a single star.
