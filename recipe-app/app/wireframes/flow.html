Where everything lives

Deliberately plain. This page fixes where things live and how they behave across the whole app, so the visual design can't move them. It starts from landing option 3 (the working prototype) and adds the rest: navigation, Our list, the log, the Kitchen, how ingredients get in, adding a recipe and settings. The numbered notes are the rules each screen has to keep.

Four places on a tab bar at the bottom, in thumb reach. Search sits just above the tab bar on Tonight. Recipe, cook mode and the story are a focused flow: the tab bar goes away, so cooking feels like a different room.

How ingredients get in: one Kitchen, five ways in

The Kitchen is the one list of what we have. Everything else reads from it or adds to it. It records have / running low / out, not quantities, because nobody weighs what's left in the bag.

aThe box arrivesOn Tuesday the Kitchen asks "Is the box in?" One tap adds this week's items, which you can review. Later the Abel & Cole email can do this on its own.
bAdd by hand"+ Add" in the Kitchen opens a search sheet with suggestions. You can add several at once, or paste a list.
cTonight's WithPicking an ingredient that isn't in the Kitchen asks "Add to the Kitchen too?" It's off by default, because tonight's choice is only for tonight.
dFrom a recipeEach ingredient shows whether you have it. Tap a missing one to say "we have it" or "add to buy".
eAfter cookingThe story's last line asks "Used up anything?" and offers the recipe's fresh ingredients to clear. Ticking a "to buy" item moves it into the Kitchen.

Decisions for you

These change where things live, so they need settling before any more visual design.

1 · Navigation model

Recommended: four tabs (Tonight · Our list · Kitchen · Log), with search above the tabs on Tonight. Each thing has one obvious home.

Alternative: keep only the search bar (round 1 Index), with Our list, Kitchen and Log behind a menu. It's calmer, but the Kitchen gets buried, and you'll need it at the fridge.

2 · What the Kitchen tracks

Recommended: have / running low / out, with staples assumed. No quantities.

Quantities ("200 g spinach left") would make "have X of Y" exact, but nobody keeps them up to date.

3 · Shopping list in v1?

If yes, screen 12 is the To buy view in the Kitchen and recipes get "Add to buy". If no, the Kitchen is a single list, and recipes only show have / need.

4 · The box

For now: a Tuesday prompt with last week's box as a checklist. Later: the Abel & Cole email adds it on its own, once the inbox is connected.

5 · Where Add a recipe lives

Recommended: on Our list, because new saves are usually things you want to cook. The share sheet from Instagram is the main way in anyway.
