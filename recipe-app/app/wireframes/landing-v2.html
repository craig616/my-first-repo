The ranking rule

  1. On our list (agreed by both, then either)
  2. Favourites (♥ by both, then either)
  3. Best match: most ingredients in, perishables used first

Every result carries a reason line in the accent colour, so the ranking never feels random. Swaps count too ("uses cod instead of tinned fish").
