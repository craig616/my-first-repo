What's in the filters: everything you'd want sometimes, kept off the landing page.

  • Who's eating lives here (the default comes from the day and stays visible in the meta line)
  • Meal includes sides, baking, breakfast and sauces: the library lives behind this, not in the recommendations
  • Show: our list, favourites, not tried yet (instead of a "not cooked yet" section), cooked before, needs checking
  • Order by: "Best for now" is the default (list → favourites → season → week → what we have)
  • The button shows a dot when filters are on, and the count updates live

Where our list lives: as a section on the page, and as "Show: our list" here, so the bottom bar stays at two elements.
