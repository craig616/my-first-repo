The week's rhythm (applies to every option)

Fresh things arrive on Tuesday (fish, tofu, vegetables, fruit), so suggestions follow the week:

Tue–Thu · Fresh first fish, tofu and veg recipes lead, using up the most perishable things first (fish before veg)
Fri–Sun · Weekend cooking longer projects move up, still using what's left
Sun–Mon · From the cupboard store-cupboard recipes lead: pasta, beans, rice, tins

The frames show Sunday evening, so they lean on the cupboard. On a Tuesday the first band would read "Fresh this week", with the fish dishes first.

How it works

  1. Speed bands (Quick · About an hour · Weekend) are the main way to decide. Weekend cooking moves to the top on Saturday and Sunday.
  2. Our list comes after the suggestions, as a compact list with who added it and when.
  3. The bottom bar: search (also "what's in the fridge"), the list, and the full library with filters.
  4. Who's eating is one quiet line, not a set of controls.

Trade-offs

+ Answers "how long have we got?" straight away. + Easy to scan. Long names wrap without breaking anything.

− Our list sits below the first screen, so job 2 takes a scroll. − Three rails can feel like a streaming app.

How it works

  1. Cook in progress appears at the very top only while timers are running.
  2. Next on our list is the hero: a large photo, with the meta line "Fish · Japanese · weeknight" (from the Cookbook route).
  3. The rest of the list sits underneath as compact rows.

Trade-offs

+ Rewards building the list together. + Calmest page: one big image and one rail. + Jobs 1 and 2 are both on the first screen.

− An empty list needs a good empty state ("Nothing lined up. Here's something quick."). − Only one speed band is visible at a time.

How it works

  1. For tonight | Our list: two plain-word tabs at the top switch the whole screen.
  2. One full-screen suggestion, with its reason first ("Quick · 30 min"). Swipe sideways through 4–5 picks; the dots show how many.
  3. Muted video can play behind the title once you pause on a card.
  4. Below the fold: the other speed bands, for browsing.

Trade-offs

+ Makes the most of the photos and video (you said they help a lot). + Feels like the Reels it came from. + Leads naturally into the full-screen recipe.

− Only one suggestion visible at a time, so it's slower to compare options. − Weak photos (some Reel frames) are exposed at full size. − Long names take up much of the image.
